A well-maintained furnace is less likely to fail on the coldest night of the year. Here are practical tips tailored for Chester County homes.

## Change Filters Regularly

Dirty filters restrict airflow and force the furnace to work harder. Check monthly and replace when dirty — especially if you have pets or allergies.

## Keep Vents Clear

Blocked supply and return vents reduce efficiency and can cause short cycling. Make sure furniture and rugs are not covering registers.

## Schedule a Fall Tune-Up

Professional maintenance includes checking the heat exchanger, testing safety controls, cleaning the burner, and verifying proper combustion. It is the single best way to prevent mid-winter failures.

## Listen for Changes

New noises, longer run times, or uneven heating are early warnings. Address them before a full breakdown leaves you without heat.

## Test Your Thermostat

Replace batteries if needed and confirm the system responds correctly to temperature changes. A simple thermostat issue can look like a major furnace problem.
