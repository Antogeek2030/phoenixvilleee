When a major HVAC component fails, the repair-or-replace decision can feel overwhelming. Here is a practical framework we use with Phoenixville homeowners.

## Age of the System

Furnaces and air conditioners typically last 12–18 years with good maintenance. If your unit is approaching or past that range and needs an expensive repair, replacement often makes more financial sense.

## Cost of the Repair

A common rule of thumb: if the repair costs more than 50% of a new system (and the unit is older), lean toward replacement. We always provide both options with clear numbers.

## Frequency of Breakdowns

Multiple repairs in a short period signal declining reliability. A new system restores peace of mind and often lowers monthly energy costs.

## Efficiency Gains

Modern high-efficiency equipment can reduce heating and cooling bills significantly compared with 15-year-old units. Factor in potential energy savings over the life of a new system.

## Comfort and Safety

Uneven temperatures, noise, or safety concerns (especially cracked heat exchangers) tip the scale toward replacement.
