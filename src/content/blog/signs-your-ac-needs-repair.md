Pennsylvania summers can be brutal. When your air conditioner struggles, comfort disappears fast. Here are five signs that mean you should schedule service before peak heat arrives.

## 1. Weak or Warm Air from Vents

If the air coming out of your vents feels lukewarm or weak, the system may be low on refrigerant, have a failing compressor, or a dirty coil. Continuing to run it can cause more damage.

## 2. Short Cycling

The unit turns on and off frequently without completing a full cooling cycle. This often points to a thermostat issue, oversized system, or refrigerant problems.

## 3. Unusual Noises

Grinding, squealing, or banging from the outdoor unit usually means a failing motor, fan blade, or loose part. Early attention prevents a complete breakdown.

## 4. Higher Electric Bills

A sudden spike in cooling costs with no change in usage often means the system is working harder than it should — dirty filters, low refrigerant, or aging components.

## 5. Water Around the Indoor Unit

Clogged condensate drains or frozen coils can cause water to leak. This can damage floors and ceilings if ignored.

If you notice any of these in your Phoenixville home, call for a same-day diagnostic. Most repairs are completed on the first visit.
